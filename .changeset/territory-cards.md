Add "Where the heat is" to the HTML report: one card per territory, hottest first, at a detail you choose with a slider (1 to 6, the recommended detail marked and shown first). A card shows the territory's share of the change effort, how many of its changes stay inside it, its hottest file, the territory it changes with most, and up to three findings (the places to start that concern it, then eroding, chronic hotspots, coupling across its edge, or many fixes). Expanding a card adds the territory against the repository, all its findings, its hottest files, the territories it changes together with, and the territories inside it with why it splits. A card links to its tile on the fit map and to its files in the treemap; test code and buckets of leftovers are drawn quieter and last. The JSON report and the flags are unchanged.
