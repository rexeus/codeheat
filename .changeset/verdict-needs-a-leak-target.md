Do not call a repository under strain on a leak that cannot be named. The verdict on the HTML report's first screen counts a territory as leaking only when at most 75 % of its changes stay inside and another territory shares changes with it (`fit.partner`), the same gate as a `boundary` place to start; a territory that keeps little inside but has no partner is left out of the judged ones. A repository with a handful of changes and no such territory now says "Not enough evidence".
